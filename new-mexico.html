Rodent Control in New Mexico

Across New Mexico, rodent problems follow climate, construction, and land use more than state lines on a map. Murvo's work here focuses on Pack rat (Neotoma spp.) and Roof rat (Rattus rattus), the species most often confirmed on inspections, in a state where desert and cactus edge, citrus and irrigation, monsoon flooding, vehicles and AC equipment, and resort and mature landscaping sustain activity around residential and commercial properties. Homes and buildings such as desert-edge single-family homes, historic adobe and older in-town homes, and homes with irrigated citrus landscaping need exclusion details matched to their materials and age, not a one-size treatment. Use the city pages linked below to find local information for your area, including common entry points, seasonal risks, and how service is delivered near you, from inspection through monitoring and follow-up.

From the first inspection, Murvo applies one consistent process across New Mexico: identify, remove, exclude, clean, and monitor. That process is backed by flat-rate pricing quoted in writing, never hourly billing, and by a written workmanship guarantee whose terms appear on your quote. Technicians are licensed and insured in New Mexico, background-checked, and trained as rodent specialists rather than general pest applicators. The city pages below explain local species, entry points, and seasonal timing in detail for the areas we serve statewide.

Every New Mexico job is priced up front at a flat rate, in writing, and backed by our workmanship guarantee - no surprises. This is why Murvo Rodent Control has built the trust of New Mexico homeowners statewide.
