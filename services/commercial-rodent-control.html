Commercial Rodent Control

A single rodent sighting can close a restaurant, fail a food-safety audit or damage stock in a warehouse. Commercial rodent control from Murvo starts with a site-wide commercial rodent inspection covering kitchens, loading docks, bin areas, storage and roof voids. We identify species, pressure points and compliance risks, then deploy monitored commercial rodent trapping and, outdoors only, locked stations linked to our controlled rodent baiting program. Service is scheduled discreetly around trading hours so customers never see disruption. Staff training pointers are included too, helping your team spot early droppings or gnawing before a small issue becomes an audit failure.

Our program follows Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor, with written service reports after every visit for your audit file. Technicians deliver commercial rodent exclusion to dock doors, vents and service penetrations, advise on proofing stores, and arrange rodent damage repair referrals for gnawed cables or stock areas. Review business options on our services page or request a commercial survey. Trend reporting shows activity falling over time, exactly what health inspectors and auditors want to see documented. Contracts can cover single sites or multiple locations, with consistent reporting formats that make group compliance reviews straightforward for managers.

For cost context, Angi's 2026 national data puts rat extermination at $396 on average, typically $189–$655, inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600 for standard jobs; commercial programs are scoped individually, and Murvo quotes a flat rate in writing before work or a clear scheduled-service price. Food premises follow strict rules: no loose rodenticide near food, locked stations only, full product records, and IPM methods that prioritize exclusion and hygiene. Licensed, insured and background-checked teams support businesses nationwide with 24/7 emergency response for urgent sightings. Emergency callouts for live sightings during trading hours are handled with particular discretion to protect your reputation with waiting customers.
