Rodenticide Application

Rodenticide application at Murvo starts with a safety warning, not a sales pitch: these products can harm pets, children and wildlife if misused, so we place them only inside tamper-resistant locked stations, never as loose pellets or open trays. A licensed technician first completes a thorough rodent inspection to confirm species, activity levels and whether rodenticide is even needed. Often professional rodent trapping plus controlled rodent baiting outdoors is enough. Signs warranting treatment include persistent droppings, burrows, gnawed wiring and repeated sightings after basic measures have failed. If trapping alone can solve your problem safely, that is the route we will recommend first, every single time without exception.

When rodenticide is justified, we follow Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor: stations are mapped, labeled, anchored and checked on schedule, with amounts recorded at each visit. We prioritize sealing entry points with rodent exclusion and physical rodent removal so dependence on chemical control shrinks quickly. Homeowners can compare approaches on our full services list and talk to Murvo first about children, pets, livestock or wildlife concerns. Dead rodents are retrieved promptly where accessible to reduce odor and secondary risks. You will receive copies of product labels and safety information for anything applied at your property, so nothing is ever hidden from you.

On cost, Angi reports in its 2026 national data that rat extermination averages $396 and commonly runs $189–$655, with inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600 — guidance only, while Murvo provides a flat rate quoted in writing before work. Regulation shapes product choice: California restricts anticoagulant rodenticides, other states impose their own limits, and we comply with every applicable rule. Treatment is backed by our written workmanship guarantee, delivered by licensed and insured, background-checked technicians nationwide, with monitoring until activity is confirmed clear. Monitoring data from each station visit guides whether treatment continues, changes product, or stops entirely in favor of proofing alone.
