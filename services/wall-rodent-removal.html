Wall Rodent Removal

Rodents in walls announce themselves with scratching, scurrying at night, faint odors and pets staring at one spot. Mice travel wall voids between kitchens and attics, while rats use them to reach food unseen. Murvo's wall rodent removal starts with a targeted rodent inspection using listening, thermal clues and entry tracing, then places rodent trapping at void access points to draw animals out without unnecessary demolition. If an animal has already died in the wall, our dead rodent removal service handles retrieval and sanitation. Our technicians carry inspection cameras for accessible voids, helping locate nests without opening large sections of plasterboard unnecessarily.

We follow Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor. Small, repairable access points are only made when truly needed and agreed with you first. Once clear, we apply rodent proofing to wall entry routes, seal baseboard and pipe gaps linked to kitchen rodent activity, and treat odors so other rodents are not attracted back. Explore related help on our services hub or contact us about wall noises today. Monitoring at key access points confirms the void is quiet before we finish. After removal, a quiet-wall check at different times of day helps confirm no second animal remains hidden in a connected void.

For price context, Angi's 2026 national data puts rat extermination at $396 on average, typically $189–$655, inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600. Murvo quotes a flat rate in writing after locating the activity, so wall access work never becomes an open-ended bill. We avoid poison for wall problems as an IPM rule, because a poisoned rodent dying in a void creates exactly the odor problem homeowners fear; trapping and exclusion are cleaner and safer around kids and pets. Work is guaranteed in writing by licensed, insured technicians nationwide. If monitoring reveals activity has moved to another void, we adjust trap positions at no confusion about what your quote covers.
