Mice Control

Mice control starts with understanding how easily mice exploit a home: a gap under a door, a hole where a cable enters, or a crack in the foundation is an open invitation. Signs include small rice-like droppings, shredded paper or insulation nests, and faint scratching after dark. Murvo builds mice programs within our wider rodent control service, often focusing on kitchen rodent control where food draws them most. Explore every supporting option on our services hub to build the right plan for your home and family. Because mice mark routes with scent, we also advise on cleaning affected cabinets and surfaces once activity ends, helping remove the trails that can attract other mice later on.

We place traps in clusters along skirting boards, behind appliances, and in attic voids, because mice travel the same short routes nightly and investigate new objects far faster than rats. Combined with thorough mouse removal, we seal entry points using whole-home rodent proofing methods and advise on food storage that removes the attraction. Follow-up visits confirm activity has stopped before we sign the job off. To get started with a same-day visit, contact Murvo and we will arrange a time that suits your household. Small details make a big difference here, from sealing behind baseboards to checking where pipes and wires pass through floors, and our technicians are trained to spot those overlooked routes.

On pricing, Angi's 2026 national data averages rat extermination at $396, typically $189 to $655, lists inspections at $100 to $200, and exclusion at $200 to $600, figures that frame most rodent work. Murvo instead gives a flat-rate written quote tailored to your mouse problem, whether it is one kitchen or the whole house. Our integrated approach favors trapping and sealing over baits, offers genuinely pet-safe options, includes dropping cleanup where needed, and is covered by a written workmanship guarantee. Combining control with careful rodent exclusion closes the circle, turning a reactive treatment into a preventive system that keeps kitchens and attics clear for good.
