Rodent Exclusion

Rodent exclusion is the work of sealing a building so rodents physically cannot enter, and it is the step that makes every other treatment last. Rats can pass through a hole the size of a quarter and mice through one far smaller, so Murvo inspects soffits, eaves, pipe entries, foundation cracks, and door sweeps in detail. Exclusion is central to our complete rodent control approach and complements broader rodent proofing measures; you can see how the pieces fit together on our services hub before booking any work for your property. We pay particular attention to junctions where different materials meet, since those seams, corners, and transitions are where gaps most often open up as a building ages and settles.

We seal with materials rodents cannot gnaw through, including copper mesh, hardware cloth, metal flashing, and quality sealants matched to each opening. Specialist work covers roof exclusion, vent exclusion, and chimney exclusion, each finished to weatherproof standards by licensed, background-checked technicians. Active animals are cleared first through trapping so none are sealed inside. If you are unsure which openings matter most, contact Murvo and we will prioritize them during an inspection visit. Before leaving, we walk the finished work with you, showing each sealed opening and explaining how it was protected, so you can see the value of the work clearly.

In terms of cost, Angi's 2026 national data lists exclusion at $200 to $600, inspections at $100 to $200, and rat extermination at $396 on average, typically $189 to $655. Murvo provides a flat-rate quote in writing covering the specific openings on your home, so you pay for the work needed, nothing more. Exclusion is also the safest long-term strategy, reducing reliance on rodenticides and supporting pet-safe, no-poison control, and every seal we install is covered by our written workmanship guarantee. Pairing exclusion with a thorough rodent inspection first ensures no hidden opening is missed, giving the finished sealing the best possible chance of lasting success.
