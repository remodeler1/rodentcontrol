Rodent Proofing

Rodent proofing goes beyond a single repair: it is a whole-property program that removes the food, shelter, and access rodents depend on. Murvo assesses your home inside and out, from overhanging branches and cluttered garages to unsealed vents and gaps under doors. Proofing works hand in hand with structural rodent exclusion and supports lasting rodent control results, especially before winter when rodents seek warmth. Browse our complete services list to see how proofing connects with inspection, removal, and ongoing monitoring options. Proofing is especially valuable for older homes, rental properties, and homes near fields, water, or dense vegetation, where rodent pressure from the surrounding area stays high all year.

Your proofing plan may include mesh screening, door sweeps, sealing utility entries, securing pet food and bird seed storage, and trimming vegetation away from walls and rooflines. Where kitchens are the draw, we coordinate with kitchen rodent control, and for storage areas, garage rodent control tackles harborage directly. Every recommendation is prioritized so essential work happens first. To arrange a proofing survey for your home, contact Murvo today and we will respond the same day wherever possible across the United States. Your written plan separates quick wins you can handle yourself from specialist sealing that needs professional materials and access equipment, keeping the whole program practical, transparent, and affordable to stage.

As a cost reference, Angi's 2026 national data puts exclusion, the core of proofing, at $200 to $600, inspections at $100 to $200, and rat extermination at an average $396, typically $189 to $655. Murvo quotes proofing as a flat rate in writing based on your property, never by the hour. Because proofing prevents infestations rather than reacting to them, it reduces the need for baits and supports pet-safe, no-poison management, and the sealing work is backed by our written workmanship guarantee. Adding regular rodent inspection checks after proofing keeps the protection current, catching new gaps early before they can turn into another indoor infestation.
