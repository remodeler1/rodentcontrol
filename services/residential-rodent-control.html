Residential Rodent Control

Residential rodent control treats the whole home, not just the room where you saw a mouse. Droppings in the kitchen, scratching in the attic and gnawing in the garage usually share one network of entry points. Murvo starts with a whole-home residential rodent inspection, then coordinates mice control and rat control as needed, using trapping first. Families, landlords and tenants across the United States use this service when single-room fixes have failed and they want one accountable team to solve it properly. A single coordinator oversees your whole-home plan, so attic, kitchen and garage findings are joined up rather than handled in isolation.

The program runs Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor across every zone: attic removal, kitchen control and garage proofing are combined with sealing, cleanup and scheduled monitoring. You can compare the building blocks on our services overview or book a home visit to get a single written plan. Background-checked technicians explain each step, keep treatments pet and child conscious, and leave a clear record of sealed points and follow-up dates. Landlords receive a separate summary of structural repairs beyond our sealing scope, making it easy to authorize follow-on building work quickly.

For guidance, Angi's 2026 national data averages rat extermination at $396, typically $189–$655, with inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600 nationally. Murvo instead gives one flat rate in writing for the whole home before work begins, so there are no per-room surprises. Our IPM approach prefers trapping and exclusion over chemicals indoors, protecting household pets and children while solving the cause. Every residential program is backed by our written workmanship guarantee and delivered by licensed, insured teams nationwide, with same-day response available. Seasonal advice is included too, because rodent pressure on homes changes noticeably between hot summers and cold winter months.
