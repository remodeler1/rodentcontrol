Rodent Waste Cleanup

Rodent waste is more than a mess. Droppings, urine trails and nesting material can carry bacteria and viruses, including hantavirus, which becomes airborne when dried waste is disturbed. That is why the first rule of safe cleanup is simple: never dry-sweep or vacuum untreated droppings. Murvo's rodent waste cleanup follows CDC-style guidance — the area is ventilated, soaked with disinfectant and left to sit before anything is touched, and technicians wear gloves, respirators and protective clothing throughout. If the waste is mainly droppings in living areas, our rodent dropping cleanup service covers the same safe process in detail.

Once disinfection has done its work, waste and contaminated materials are bagged, sealed and removed, and surfaces are disinfected again before a final rodent disinfection treatment tackles lingering bacteria and odor-causing residue. In attics and crawl spaces we frequently pair cleanup with attic rodent removal or crawl space rodent removal, since waste accumulates where rodents nest. Cleanup only makes sense once the animals are gone and entry points are sealed, so we coordinate with rodent exclusion as part of one plan, which you can explore on our services page.

Cleanup is priced as a flat rate quoted in writing after we assess the volume and location of contamination. For budgeting context, Angi's 2026 national data puts inspections at $100–$200 and rat extermination at $396 on average, typically $189–$655, while cleanup costs scale with how much material must be removed. We never pressure you into work the space does not need, and our written workmanship guarantee covers the cleanup standard we promise. Treating waste safely also protects children and pets who share the home, which is why we never cut corners on PPE or dwell time. To book safely, contact Murvo today.
