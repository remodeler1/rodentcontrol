Crawl Space Rodent Removal

Rodents love crawl spaces: dark, sheltered and close to pipes and warmth. Signs you need crawl space rodent removal include droppings under the house, torn vapor barrier, chewed pipe insulation, burrows at foundation vents and smells drifting up through floors. Murvo begins with a under-home rodent inspection, then uses targeted crawl space rodent trapping to remove active animals. Because activity below often connects to rodents in the attic via wall voids, we trace the full route rather than treating one area in isolation. Standing water, leaking pipes and stored timber under a house all increase rodent pressure, so we flag those issues during inspection too.

Our sequence is Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor. After clearance, technicians deliver vent rodent exclusion with sturdy mesh, seal pier and plumbing gaps as part of wider rodent exclusion work, and complete crawl space waste cleanup to remove droppings and nesting. See how the steps fit together on our services page or request a crawl space visit. Monitoring checks confirm activity has ended before we close the job, and photos document every sealed opening for your records. Access panels are refitted securely after every visit, keeping the space closed to neighborhood cats and other unwanted wildlife as well.

Cost guidance from Angi's 2026 national data: rat extermination averages $396, typically $189–$655, inspections run $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600 nationally. Murvo works differently, providing a flat rate quoted in writing before any work, with no surprises if extra vents need sealing within the agreed scope. We favor trapping and physical exclusion over poison under homes, an IPM approach that protects pets that may access the crawl space and avoids carcasses in hidden areas. All work carries our written workmanship guarantee from licensed, insured technicians. Photographs of the finished sealing are supplied with your guarantee paperwork for complete peace of mind.
