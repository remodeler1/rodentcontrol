Garage Rodent Control

Garages are a rodent gateway: gaps under side doors, clutter for shelter and direct access into wall voids leading indoors. Signs you need garage rodent control include droppings along walls, shredded storage boxes, nests in corners and chewed vehicle wiring or hoses. Murvo begins with a garage rodent inspection of doors, corners and stored items, then deploys garage rodent trapping along runways. If activity has spread, we coordinate whole-home rodent removal and check adjoining attic rodent entry points above the garage. Bird seed, grass seed and pet food stored in garages are powerful rodent magnets, so we check storage containers during every inspection visit.

Lasting results follow Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor. We fit door sweeps and corner seals through garage rodent proofing, mesh weep holes and vents via full rodent exclusion, and clear droppings and nesting so smells do not lure newcomers. Homeowners can see every related service on our services list or arrange a garage visit. Where outdoor pressure is high, locked bait stations may supplement trapping outside the garage, always placed with pet and child safety first and fully explained beforehand. We also check attic hatches above garages, a commonly missed route that lets rodents move from parked cars straight into bedrooms.

Budget guidance from Angi's 2026 national data: rat extermination averages $396, typically $189–$655, inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600. Murvo's own price is a flat rate quoted in writing before work, covering removal, sealing and monitoring, and backed by a written workmanship guarantee. Our IPM approach stresses decluttering, sealed storage bins and removing pet food from garages, reducing shelter so exclusion holds long term. Licensed and insured technicians provide garage control across the United States, with same-day response available in many areas. Seasonal checks before winter are popular, as rodents actively seek warm garage shelter when outdoor temperatures begin to drop sharply.
