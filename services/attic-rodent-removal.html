Attic Rodent Removal

Scratching at night, droppings on insulation, chewed wires and a musty smell are classic signs you need attic rodent removal. Rats and roof rats climb to rooflines, while mice slip through vent gaps, and both contaminate insulation and create fire risks by gnawing cables. Murvo starts with a detailed rodent inspection of the attic, roof and eaves, then sets humane, targeted rodent trapping in the attic to clear active animals quickly. We also check for young, nests and secondary entry points in adjoining crawl spaces so nothing is missed. Photographs from inside the attic help you see the evidence for yourself before you approve any removal or sealing work.

Removal is only step two of Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor. Once the attic is clear, we deliver roofline rodent exclusion and general rodent proofing with metal mesh and sealed flashing, then arrange attic dropping cleanup and disinfection where contamination is present. Browse the full process on our services overview or book an attic visit today. Monitoring traps stay briefly after sealing to confirm no animal is trapped inside and no new activity appears. We also advise on trimming overhanging branches and clearing roof debris that give climbing rats an easy bridge onto your roof.

For budgeting, Angi's 2026 national data lists rat extermination at $396 on average, usually $189–$655, inspections at $100–$200 and exclusion work at $200–$600. Those are national benchmarks, not Murvo prices: we inspect, then give one flat rate in writing before work begins, backed by a written workmanship guarantee. Our IPM approach favors trapping and exclusion over poison in attics, protecting pets and preventing animals dying in inaccessible voids. Licensed and insured teams provide this service nationwide, with same-day response in most cases. Same-day attic visits are prioritized when chewed wiring is found, because that damage can create a genuine fire risk overnight.
