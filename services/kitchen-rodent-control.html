Kitchen Rodent Control

Kitchens draw rodents with food, water and warm hiding spots behind appliances. Signs you need kitchen rodent control include droppings in drawers, gnawed packaging, grease marks along kickboards and scratching under units at night. Murvo starts with a kitchen-focused rodent inspection behind fridges, ovens and sinks, then sets discreet rodent trapping in kitchens away from food preparation surfaces. Because kitchen activity often starts elsewhere, we also trace routes from rodents in wall voids and basements before treating the symptom alone. Night-time trail cameras are sometimes used in tricky kitchen cases to pinpoint exactly which gap a cautious mouse is traveling through.

Our Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor process seals the real entry points: gaps around waste pipes, holes behind cabinets and spaces under doors, using food-area rodent proofing and wider mice control for the whole home where needed. Contaminated areas receive rodent disinfection guidance and cleanup. View all household options on our services page or ask about kitchen treatment. We use no loose poison in kitchens, keeping children, pets and food completely away from rodenticides. We leave simple written hygiene notes tailored to your kitchen layout, covering bins, pet bowls and gaps behind rarely moved appliances.

On pricing, Angi's 2026 national data averages rat extermination at $396, commonly $189–$655, with inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600 — national guidance only. Murvo quotes one flat rate in writing before work, covering trapping, sealing and follow-up monitoring, backed by our written workmanship guarantee. Our IPM advice covers food storage in sealed containers, fixing drips and clearing crumbs behind appliances, small changes that make exclusion last. Licensed, insured and background-checked technicians deliver kitchen control nationwide, often with same-day response. Same-day kitchen visits are available when droppings are found near food preparation areas and hygiene simply cannot wait overnight.
