Same-Day Rodent Control

Same-day rodent control is for the moment a rodent problem stops being a suspicion and becomes urgent: droppings on the kitchen counter this morning, scratching inside a wall at 2 a.m., or a rat spotted crossing the garage. Those signs mean rodents are already sheltering, feeding and breeding indoors, and waiting a week lets a small problem multiply. Murvo treats urgent calls as the priority they are, dispatching background-checked technicians the same day across the United States. If the activity sounds severe, our 24/7 emergency rodent removal team can advise you on immediate steps while your technician is on the way.

Your visit starts with a thorough rodent inspection that maps entry points, nesting areas, food and water sources, and the travel routes rodents use along walls and pipes. From there our rodent removal work begins, using targeted rodent trapping and, where appropriate, carefully placed baiting, chosen around children, pets and non-target wildlife. We then seal the openings that let them in through rodent exclusion, clean contaminated areas, and set monitoring so any new activity is caught early. You can compare the full range of options on our rodent control services page before or after your visit.

Pricing stays simple: Murvo works to a flat rate quoted in writing before work starts, so there are no hourly surprises. For context, Angi's 2026 national figures put rat extermination at $396 on average, usually between $189 and $655, which helps you judge any quote sensibly. Our approach follows integrated pest management, using the least hazardous effective method first and reserving chemical treatments for where they are genuinely needed. Every same-day job is backed by our written workmanship guarantee, and if activity returns within the guarantee terms we come back. Ready to act today? Contact Murvo and ask for same-day availability in your area.
