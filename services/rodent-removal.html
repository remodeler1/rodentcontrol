Rodent Removal

Rodent removal means clearing the animals already living in your home, not simply scattering them with repellents or poison alone. Murvo handles rats and mice in attics, wall voids, garages, and kitchens, starting with a careful assessment of species, numbers, and access routes. That assessment shapes whether we recommend targeted rodent trapping, attic rodent removal, or work in other areas, and you can compare every option on our full services page before deciding how to proceed with confidence. Technicians wear protective equipment, handle all waste safely, and leave treated areas tidy, because removal should solve a health concern rather than create another one indoors.

Once you approve the written flat-rate quote, our background-checked technicians set traps along proven runways, check them on schedule, and remove animals promptly and hygienically. For wall voids we also offer specialist wall rodent removal, and for outbuildings, garage rodent control that tackles stored-food and clutter harborage. Removal is immediately followed by entry-point exclusion so new rodents cannot replace those removed. Questions at any stage? Just contact Murvo and we will talk you through the plan. Throughout the job we keep children and pets away from working areas, explain each stage in plain language, and confirm exactly when rooms and attics are safe to use again.

For budgeting, Angi reports in its 2026 national data that rat extermination averages $396, typically ranging from $189 to $655, with inspections between $100 and $200 and exclusion between $200 and $600. Murvo instead quotes one flat rate in writing for your specific home, so cost reflects your infestation, not a national average. We use integrated pest management and pet-safe, no-poison methods first where practical, arrange rodent disinfection after heavy activity, and stand behind the result with a written workmanship guarantee. Choosing professional rodent trapping as part of removal also gives measurable results, since catch records show progress clearly instead of relying on guesswork or hope.
