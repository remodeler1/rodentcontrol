Rodent Damage Repair

Rodents damage homes quietly and constantly. Rats gnaw joists, pipes and electrical wiring — a recognized fire risk — while mice shred insulation for nesting and contaminate far more than they eat. By the time you notice sagging insulation, a musty smell or a chewed cable, an active infestation is usually still present above or below you. Murvo's rodent damage repair starts by confirming the animals are gone through our rodent removal service, because repairing damage while rodents remain simply gives them fresh material to destroy. We then document every affected area so you can see exactly what needs restoring.

Repairs are matched to the damage: contaminated insulation is removed and replaced, gnawed wood is reinforced or renewed, chewed screens and vents are refitted, and damaged ducting is made good. Crucially, every repair visit doubles as prevention, pairing the restoration with rodent proofing and full rodent exclusion so the same holes are never reused. Where droppings or nesting have contaminated the space, we recommend rodent disinfection before new materials go in. Attic jobs often pair naturally with attic rodent removal, and you can review everything we offer on our services page.

Murvo prices repairs as a flat rate quoted in writing before work begins, itemized so you can prioritize urgent items like wiring risks. As a benchmark, Angi's 2026 national data averages rat extermination at $396, generally $189–$655, with exclusion work commonly $200–$600 — useful context when weighing repair quotes. We work to integrated pest management principles, fixing the conditions that attracted rodents rather than just the symptoms, and all repair workmanship carries our written guarantee. If you are unsure how extensive your damage is, contact Murvo to arrange an assessment and a clear, itemized written quote.
