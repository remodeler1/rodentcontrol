Emergency Rodent Removal

Some rodent problems cannot wait: a live rat in a child's bedroom, a mouse loose in a restaurant kitchen, or scratching and a strong odor suggesting an animal trapped indoors. Emergency rodent removal is Murvo's round-the-clock rapid response, with technicians available at night, weekends and holidays across the United States. Call and we dispatch for same-day rodent control triage, talk you through keeping people and pets in a separate room, and arrive equipped for safe capture. We also handle urgent dead rodent removal when smell or health risk makes delay unacceptable.

On arrival we Inspect, Remove, Exclude, Clean and Monitor in emergency order: capture or trap the animal first, check for others using our emergency rodent inspection, temporarily secure the entry route, and disinfect the affected area. Full permanent rodent exclusion and complete rodent removal are then scheduled promptly, often the next day. See our wider emergency and standard services or contact the 24/7 team now if you are reading this during an incident — do not try to corner a wild rodent yourself. If children or elderly relatives are in the house, tell us on the call so the technician can prioritize their rooms first.

For cost context, Angi's 2026 national data averages rat extermination at $396, typically $189–$655, inspections $100–$200 and exclusion $200–$600; emergency attendance is scoped on the call, and Murvo confirms a flat rate in writing before work beyond the immediate removal. Night work follows the same safety rules: trapping and capture first, no loose poison in living spaces, and extra caution around children and pets woken by the disturbance. Every emergency job is backed by our written workmanship guarantee from licensed, insured technicians, day or night. You will know exactly who is attending, because our call handler confirms the technician's name before dispatch wherever possible.
