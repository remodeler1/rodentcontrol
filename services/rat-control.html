Rat Control

Rat control demands a different approach from mice: rats are cautious, strong, and quick to learn, so poorly placed traps or scattered bait rarely solve the problem. Murvo identifies the species, maps travel routes along fences, pipes, and rooflines, and targets harborage in attics, basements, and outbuildings. Rat work is a core part of our nationwide rodent control service, and urgent cases can use emergency rodent removal at any hour. See all related options on our services page for homes and businesses alike. We also check drains, vents, and outbuildings, because rats frequently travel between a main house and nearby structures, and missing one harborage can undermine an otherwise thorough control program.

Our technicians pre-bait unset traps so wary rats feed confidently, then set them along proven runways, checking and adjusting until activity stops. We pair trapping with complete rat removal and follow with roofline exclusion where roof rats enter, plus attic rodent removal and cleanup when nests are found overhead. Every visit is documented so you can see progress clearly. For a same-day start, contact Murvo now and talk to a real specialist, day or night. Safety briefings cover trap locations, hygiene around droppings, and what to expect between visits, so everyone in the household knows how to help the program succeed from day one.

For price context, Angi's 2026 national data averages rat extermination at $396, typically $189 to $655, with inspections at $100 to $200 and exclusion at $200 to $600. Murvo works differently, quoting a flat rate in writing after assessing your infestation, so a small attic problem is not priced like a whole-house one. We prioritize trapping and exclusion within an integrated program, offer no-poison options where suitable, keep any stations tamper-resistant and away from pets, and guarantee our workmanship in writing. Finishing with proper rodent exclusion is essential, because without sealing, even excellent trapping only buys time before another group discovers the same easy way inside.
