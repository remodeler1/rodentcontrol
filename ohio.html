Rodent Control in Ohio

Across Ohio, rodent problems follow climate, construction, and land use more than state lines on a map. Murvo's work here focuses on Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), the species most often confirmed on inspections, in a state where deep winter cold, alley and sewer systems, lake and river corridors, older foundations, and summer construction and restaurant activity sustain activity around residential and commercial properties. Homes and buildings such as vintage two-flats and greystones, brick bungalows, older single-family homes, and farm and rural properties need exclusion details matched to their materials and age, not a one-size treatment.
