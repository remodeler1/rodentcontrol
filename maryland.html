Rodent Control in Maryland

Rodent control across Maryland has to account for real regional differences, and Murvo builds state coverage around them. The species mix here centers on Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), each using buildings differently, while pressure is driven by alleys and shared walls, old sewers, river and harbor corridors, restaurant waste, and student and renter turnover. Housing across the state, including historic rowhomes, brick twins, older apartments, and wooded suburban single-family homes, creates varied entry points, harborage, and access challenges from dense cities to rural-edge towns.
