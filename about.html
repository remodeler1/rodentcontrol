Who We Are — Why Murvo Does Rodents, and Only Rodents

Murvo Rodent Control was built around one idea: rodents are not a side service. Rats and mice chew wiring, contaminate food, spread disease and breed faster than general pest plans are designed to handle, so we specialize in the full chain — inspection, removal, exclusion, cleanup and damage repair — instead of spraying and hoping.

Our technicians are licensed, insured and background-checked, and they are trained on rodent behavior first: how Norway rats burrow, how roof rats travel rooflines, and how house mice exploit gaps the width of a dime. That specialism shows in the work — from attic rodent removal and crawl space removal to roof exclusion, vent covers and chimney caps.

Two promises run every job. First, flat-rate pricing quoted in writing before work begins — no hourly meters, no surprise add-ons. Second, a written workmanship guarantee on what we do, whether that is rat removal for a homeowner or ongoing commercial rodent control for a restaurant group. When something goes wrong at 2 a.m., our emergency team answers. Read our Terms of Service for exactly how those promises work.

Our approach follows the U.S. EPA’s integrated pest management principles and the CDC’s guidance on rodent-borne disease and safe cleanup — exclusion first, targeted treatment, and cleanup that never dry-sweeps droppings into the air.

Our Story — Built to Do One Thing Well

Murvo Rodent Control was built around a simple idea: rodents are a specialist problem, and they deserve a specialist answer. General pest companies split their attention across dozens of pests; Murvo does rodents and only rodents — rats, mice and the damage they leave behind — so every inspection, exclusion material and cleanup protocol we use is chosen for rodent behavior specifically.

rodentcontrol.us is the official website of Murvo Rodent Control, the licensed and insured provider behind every service described on this site. When you call (888) 842-2618, you are dealing directly with Murvo — there is no separate parent company, franchise layer or call-centre reseller between you and the crew that shows up at your door.

Our approach is exclusion-first: find how rodents are getting in, seal those entry points with rodent-proof materials, remove the animals already inside, and clean up the contamination they leave. That is the model we run in every one of the 13,822 cities we serve across 48 states and the District of Columbia.

Our team

Meet the Team Behind Murvo

Every Murvo job is handled by licensed, background-checked rodent specialists — not generalist exterminators rotating through pest types. Our technicians are trained in rodent biology, exclusion construction (sealing rooflines, vents, chimneys and foundations), humane trapping, and the safe cleanup of droppings and nesting material following CDC guidance. Behind them, our dispatch team answers calls 24/7 and matches each job to a local crew with the right equipment for the species involved — from Norway rats in northern basements to roof rats in southern attics and pack rats in desert garages.

The Murvo team also researches and writes every page on this website, drawing on CDC and EPA guidance, National Pest Management Association resources, and state regulations — see our Editorial Policy for how our content is researched and reviewed.
