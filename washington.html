Rodent Control in Washington

Murvo provides rodent control throughout Washington, with city pages below for the communities served in each region. Statewide, the recurring problems involve Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), supported by sustained rain, crawlspaces, waterways and waterfront, hillside burrows, older housing, and compost and garden food sources that keep food, water, and shelter close to homes and businesses. The building stock matters just as much: Craftsman and older single-family homes with crawlspaces, hillside homes, and waterfront properties dominates many areas and shapes how rodents enter, travel, and nest. Rather than repeat one generic message, each linked city page explains its own species pressure, entry points, and service considerations, so you can start with the location closest to your property and compare local considerations side by side.

Washington pest control is licensed through the Washington State Department of Agriculture (WSDA), with rule changes noted for January 2026 to verify before publishing method-specific claims. Murvo delivers that standard through local crews serving communities across Washington, so statewide coverage still means a technician who understands your housing and species mix. Each property receives a documented inspection, targeted removal, durable exclusion, cleanup where needed, and follow-up monitoring, all covered by one flat-rate written quote and a written guarantee. We favor trapping and exclusion, use bait only in locked stations where appropriate, and explain safety precautions in advance. Select a city page below to see coverage near you.

Every Washington job is priced up front at a flat rate, in writing, and backed by our workmanship guarantee - no surprises. This is why Murvo Rodent Control has earned the trust of Washington residents statewide.
