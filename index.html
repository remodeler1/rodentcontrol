Rat & Mouse Control Built Around Exclusion, Not Just Traps

Hearing scratching in the walls at night or finding droppings along the baseboards is usually the first sign — and rarely the only one. Murvo Rodent Control is a rodent-only specialist: every visit starts with a full rodent inspection that identifies the species, the entry points and the conditions drawing rodents in, because rat control for a roof rat in the attic looks nothing like mice control for house mice behind the stove.

From there we remove the animals already inside with targeted rodent trapping or carefully managed rodent baiting, then do the part most companies skip: rodent proofing and full rodent exclusion that seals gaps as small as a dime. Attics, crawl spaces and walls each get their own plan, and when the mess needs attention we handle dropping cleanup and disinfection too.

Every job — from residential rodent control for a single-family home to commercial rodent control for restaurants and warehouses — is priced flat-rate and quoted in writing before work begins, with a written workmanship guarantee. Need help tonight? Our 24/7 emergency rodent removal and same-day rodent control teams are on call around the clock.
