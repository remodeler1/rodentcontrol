Rodent Control in Arizona

Homeowners searching for rodent control in Arizona need local answers, not a national script. In this state, Pack rat (Neotoma spp.) and Roof rat (Rattus rattus) account for most calls, and activity is shaped by desert and cactus edge, citrus and irrigation, monsoon flooding, vehicles and AC equipment, and resort and mature landscaping alongside the shelter found in desert-edge single-family homes, historic adobe and older in-town homes, and homes with irrigated citrus landscaping. Attics, crawl spaces, garages, and wall voids all play a role depending on the community. This state hub gives the statewide picture, including how Murvo works and what regulation affects service, while the city pages below carry the detailed, location-specific guidance for each area covered across Arizona. Each page is written for that community, not copied from a neighboring town or city.

Statewide service works because the standard does not change when the city does.
