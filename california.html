Rodent Control in California

Across California, rodent problems follow climate, construction, and land use more than state lines on a map. Murvo's work here focuses on Roof rat (Rattus rattus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), the species most often confirmed on inspections, in a state where rain, palms and citrus, hillside and canyon edges, restaurant density, drought and wildfire displacement, and older housing stock sustain activity around residential and commercial properties. Homes and buildings such as bungalows and ranch homes, Victorians and stucco row homes, hillside homes, and suburban homes near canyons and open space need exclusion details matched to their materials and age, not a one-size treatment. Use the city pages linked below to find local information for your area, including common entry points, seasonal risks, and how service is delivered near you, from inspection through monitoring and follow-up.

California bars most anticoagulant rodenticide use under AB 1788 (2020), AB 1322 (2023), and AB 2552 (2024), and pest control is regulated by the Structural Pest Control Board and DPR, so programs must lead with exclusion, trapping, and non-anticoagulant methods. From the first inspection, Murvo applies one consistent process across California: identify, remove, exclude, clean, and monitor. That process is backed by flat-rate pricing quoted in writing, never hourly billing, and by a written workmanship guarantee whose terms appear on your quote. Technicians are licensed and insured in California, background-checked, and trained as rodent specialists rather than general pest applicators. The city pages below explain local species, entry points, and seasonal timing in detail for the areas we serve statewide.

Each California job is priced up front at a flat rate, in writing, and protected by our workmanship guarantee - no hidden fees. That's how Murvo Rodent Control has earned the trust of California homeowners statewide.
