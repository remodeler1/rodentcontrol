Rodent Control in Michigan

Homeowners searching for rodent control in Michigan need local answers, not a national script. In this state, Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus) account for most calls, and activity is shaped by deep winter cold, alley and sewer systems, lake and river corridors, older foundations, and summer construction and restaurant activity alongside the shelter found in vintage two-flats and greystones, brick bungalows, older single-family homes, and farm and rural properties. Attics, crawl spaces, garages, and wall voids all play a role depending on the community.
