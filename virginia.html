Rodent Control in Virginia

Homeowners searching for rodent control in Virginia need local answers, not a national script. In this state, Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus) account for most calls, and activity is shaped by alleys and shared walls, old sewers, river and harbor corridors, restaurant waste, and student and renter turnover alongside the shelter found in historic rowhomes, brick twins, older apartments, and wooded suburban single-family homes. Attics, crawl spaces, garages, and wall voids all play a role depending on the community. This state hub gives the statewide picture, including how Murvo works and what regulation affects service, while the city pages below carry the detailed, location-specific guidance for each area covered across Virginia.
