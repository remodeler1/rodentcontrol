Rodent Control in Connecticut

Murvo provides rodent control throughout Connecticut, with city pages below for the communities served in each region. Statewide, the recurring problems involve Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), supported by first freeze, old sewers, restaurant and waste density, shared walls, and constant construction displacement that keep food, water, and shelter close to homes and businesses. The building stock matters just as much: pre-war rowhomes, brownstones, triple-deckers, and older single-family homes dominates many areas and shapes how rodents enter, travel, and nest. Rather than repeat one generic message, each linked city page explains its own species pressure, entry points, and service considerations, so you can start with the location closest to your property and compare local considerations side by side.

Murvo delivers that standard through local crews serving communities across Connecticut, so statewide coverage still means a technician who understands your housing and species mix. Each property receives a documented inspection, targeted removal, durable exclusion, cleanup where needed, and follow-up monitoring, all covered by one flat-rate written quote and a written guarantee. We favor trapping and exclusion, use bait only in locked stations where appropriate, and explain safety precautions in advance. Select a city page below to see coverage near you.

Every Connecticut job is priced up front at a flat rate, in writing, and protected by our workmanship guarantee - no guesswork. This is how Murvo Rodent Control has built the trust of Connecticut residents statewide.
