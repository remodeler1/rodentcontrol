Rodent Control in New York

Across New York, rodent problems follow climate, construction, and land use more than state lines on a map. Murvo's work here focuses on Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), the species most often confirmed on inspections, in a state where first freeze, old sewers, restaurant and waste density, shared walls, and constant construction displacement sustain activity around residential and commercial properties. Homes and buildings such as pre-war rowhomes, brownstones, triple-deckers, and older single-family homes need exclusion details matched to their materials and age, not a one-size treatment.
