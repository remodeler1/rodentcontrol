Almost every job starts with a rodent inspection. It identifies the species, how they are getting in and how established the infestation is — which determines whether you need trapping, exclusion, cleanup or a combination.

Removal takes out the rodents already inside, using trapping or carefully managed baiting. Exclusion seals the entry points so new rodents cannot get in. Removal without exclusion usually means the problem returns, which is why Murvo builds both into most plans.
