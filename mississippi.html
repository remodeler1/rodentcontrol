Rodent Control in Mississippi

Murvo provides rodent control throughout Mississippi, with city pages below for the communities served in each region. Statewide, the recurring problems involve Roof rat (Rattus rattus) and Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus), supported by mature tree canopy, crawlspaces, flooding and hurricanes, fruit trees, and restaurant and warehouse food waste that keep food, water, and shelter close to homes and businesses. The building stock matters just as much: bungalows and ranch homes with crawlspaces, historic homes, and suburban homes under mature tree canopy dominates many areas and shapes how rodents enter, travel, and nest.
