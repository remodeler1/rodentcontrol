Editorial policy

How We Research, Write and Review Our Content

This page explains how the content on rodentcontrol.us is produced, so you can judge for yourself how much to trust it. It covers our sources, who writes and reviews our pages, how we handle pricing data and imagery, and how to get something corrected.

Who writes and reviews our content

Every page on this website is written and reviewed by the Murvo Rodent Control team — licensed rodent specialists and the dispatchers who talk to customers every day. We do not buy articles from content farms, and we do not publish service or location pages for places we do not serve: every city page on this site corresponds to a real place in the United States, verified against US Census Bureau and USGS place records.

Sources we rely on

  • CDC — rodent-borne disease, hantavirus and safe droppings cleanup guidance.
  • EPA — integrated pest management (IPM) principles and pesticide safety.
  • National Pest Management Association (NPMA) — industry research and market data.
  • State and local regulations — for example, state rodenticide rules that affect how baiting may be done in your state.
  • Angi cost data — the national price ranges quoted on this site are benchmark ranges from published cost research, clearly labeled as such. Your actual Murvo price is always one flat rate quoted in writing after inspection.

Accuracy and updates

Species, seasonality and housing guidance on our location pages is written per region — the rodents, entry points and peak seasons in Chicago are not the same as in Phoenix or Miami, and our pages say so. We review content when CDC, EPA or state guidance changes and when our own service data shows a pattern has shifted. Pages show a last-updated date in the footer.

Imagery disclosure

Some photography on this website is generated imagery that illustrates our services, equipment and the neighborhoods we serve. It is illustrative only: it does not depict specific customers, their homes, or completed jobs. Our logo and service images are used consistently so you can recognise genuine Murvo pages.

Reviews

Customer reviews published on this site carry the reviewer name and service shown with them. Our 4.9-star average rating is calculated from the reviews published on this website.

Corrections

If you spot an error — a wrong fact, a broken link, a place name that looks off — call us on (888) 842-2618 and we will check it and correct the page if it is wrong.
