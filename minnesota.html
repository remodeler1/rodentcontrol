Rodent Control in Minnesota

Rodent control across Minnesota has to account for real regional differences, and Murvo builds state coverage around them. The species mix here centers on Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) and House mouse (Mus musculus), each using buildings differently, while pressure is driven by deep winter cold, alley and sewer systems, lake and river corridors, older foundations, and summer construction and restaurant activity. Housing across the state, including vintage two-flats and greystones, brick bungalows, older single-family homes, and farm and rural properties, creates varied entry points, harborage, and access challenges from dense cities to rural-edge towns. This hub connects you to individual city pages below, where local drivers, housing, seasonal timing, and pricing factors are covered in specific detail for each community we serve. Every job is quoted flat-rate in writing before work begins, with a written guarantee on the work performed. Murvo works statewide using the same inspection-led, exclusion-first standard: confirm the species, remove the animals inside, seal entry points with gnaw-resistant materials, clean contaminated areas, and monitor until activity stops. Crews are licensed and insured in Minnesota, follow integrated pest management, and offer no-poison options where they fit.
