Privacy Policy — In Plain Language

This Privacy Policy explains what information Murvo Rodent Control collects when you call us or use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain on purpose: we are a rodent control company, not a data business, and we do not sell your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Murvo by phone or through our contact page, we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, email address if you provide one, the property address, and what you tell us about the rodent problem — noises, droppings, damage or smells. When you book a service, we also keep records of your quotes, appointments, the work performed and your guarantee paperwork. Our website is a static site; like most websites it generates standard server logs that may include your browser type, device and the pages you visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, recommend and schedule the right service, provide written flat-rate quotes, carry out the work, honor your written workmanship guarantee, and follow up about the service you received. We may also use it to improve our services and this website. We do not use your information for unrelated purposes.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver your service — for example with the licensed local crew assigned to your job — with service providers who support our operations under confidentiality obligations, and where we are required to by law or to protect safety.

4. Cookies & Website Data

This website may use essential cookies needed for it to function, and standard analytics or server logs that help us understand which pages are useful. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work, and you can always reach us by phone at (888) 842-2618.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we limit access to the people who need your details to serve you.

6. Your Choices & Rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to retain it for guarantee, safety or legal reasons. You can also tell us not to contact you about anything other than your booked service. To make any of these requests, call (888) 842-2618.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are for property owners and managers, and this website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page. Continuing to use the website or our services after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your information? Call Murvo Rodent Control at (888) 842-2618 or reach us through our contact page. See also our Terms of Service and our rodent services.
