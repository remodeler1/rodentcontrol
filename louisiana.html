Rodent Control in Louisiana

Homeowners searching for rodent control in Louisiana need local answers, not a national script. In this state, Roof rat (Rattus rattus) and Norway rat (Rattus norvegicus) account for most calls, and activity is shaped by mature tree canopy, crawlspaces, flooding and hurricanes, fruit trees, and restaurant and warehouse food waste alongside the shelter found in bungalows and ranch homes with crawlspaces, historic homes, and suburban homes under mature tree canopy. Attics, crawl spaces, garages, and wall voids all play a role depending on the community. This state hub gives the statewide picture, including how Murvo works and what regulation affects service, while the city pages below carry the detailed, location-specific guidance for each area covered across Louisiana. Each page is written for that community, not copied from a neighboring town or city.

Statewide service works because the standard does not change when the city does. Murvo inspects before quoting, prioritizes exclusion over repeated trapping, handles droppings cleanup with disinfect-first methods, and monitors to confirm results. Every customer in Louisiana receives the same protections: a flat-rate price in writing before work starts, a written guarantee, and licensed, insured crews following integrated pest management principles.
