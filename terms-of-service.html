Terms of Service — In Plain Language

These Terms of Service explain how Murvo Rodent Control provides rodent control services, how our flat-rate quotes and written workmanship guarantee work, and what we ask of you as a customer. They are written in plain language on purpose.

1. Acceptance of These Terms

By using the Murvo Rodent Control website or booking any Murvo service, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or our services. These terms apply to residential and commercial customers across the United States.

2. Services Described

Murvo provides rodent control services including inspection, removal, trapping, baiting, exclusion and proofing, attic, crawl-space, wall, kitchen and garage rodent work, cleanup, disinfection, odor removal, dead rodent removal, damage repair, and emergency and same-day response. Service availability, methods and products used may vary by property, infestation and applicable state or local regulation, and your technician will explain the plan for your property before work begins.

3. Estimates & Flat-Rate Quotes

Every job is priced as a flat rate quoted in writing before work begins. Your written quote describes the work included and the total price for that work. If conditions found during the job materially change the scope — for example, hidden damage or additional entry points — we will explain the change and obtain your approval for a revised quote before doing any additional work. National cost figures referenced on this website are third-party benchmarks provided for context only and are not Murvo prices.

4. Guarantee Terms

Murvo backs its work with a written workmanship guarantee, the terms of which are stated on your quote or service paperwork. In general, if rodents re-enter through an entry point we sealed, or work we performed fails due to our workmanship, we will return and put it right at no additional charge for that work. The guarantee does not cover new entry points created after our visit, damage caused by other trades, weather or animals, areas outside the quoted scope, or reinfestation resulting from conditions we recommended fixing that were declined.

5. Scheduling & Cancellations

Appointments are scheduled through our dispatch team and confirmed by phone. Arrival windows are estimates; traffic, weather and prior jobs can shift timing, and we will keep you updated. If you need to reschedule or cancel, please call (888) 842-2618 as early as possible. Emergency and same-day service is provided subject to crew availability.

6. Customer Responsibilities

You agree to provide safe, reasonable access to all areas included in your quote, including attics, crawl spaces, garages and utility areas. Please follow any preparation instructions we provide, secure or remove valuables from work areas, and keep children and pets away from treated areas, traps and equipment until your technician confirms it is safe. You are responsible for telling us about known hazards on the property, including asbestos, structural damage or aggressive animals.

7. Payments

Payment is due as stated on your quote or invoice. We accept the payment methods confirmed by dispatch at booking. Quoted prices include the work described; additional services requested or approved during a visit will be quoted before they are performed.

8. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Murvo Rodent Control's liability arising from any service is limited to the amount you paid for that service. Rodent control reduces and manages rodent activity; no pest control provider can guarantee that a property will remain permanently free of rodents, because new entry points and environmental conditions change over time. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

The content on rodentcontrol.us — including text, logos, images and layout — belongs to Murvo Rodent Control or its licensors and may not be copied or reused without written permission. You may use this website for personal, non-commercial purposes and to book services. Information on this website is general guidance, not a substitute for an on-site inspection.

10. Privacy Summary

We collect the information you give us when you call or submit a form — such as your name, phone number, email, ZIP code and a description of your rodent problem — and use it to schedule and provide services, prepare quotes and follow up on your job. We do not sell your personal information. Contact us if you would like to review or delete information we hold about you.

11. Changes to These Terms

We may update these Terms of Service from time to time. The version published on this page at the time of your booking is the version that applies to that booking. Continued use of the website after changes are published means you accept the updated terms.

12. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the United States and of the state in which the services are performed, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Any dispute will be handled in the courts of competent jurisdiction for the location where the services were provided.
